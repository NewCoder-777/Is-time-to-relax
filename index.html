It's time to relax!

Did you know that walking, running, playing, and participating in many other outdoors activities can greatly benefit your health?
Some of the most important benefits include relaxation, improved mental well-being, and better sleep.

Adults are not the only ones who benefit from spending time outdoors; children also gain many benefits from playing outside. These benefits range from improved physical health to better behavior and mental well-being.
The organization HealthyChildren.org hightlights many of these advantages.
